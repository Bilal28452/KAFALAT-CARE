What is KafalatCare

Compassionate Care
Meets Intelligent Technology

KafalatCare is a comprehensive digital platform that modernises how orphanages manage children's records, health, education, donations, sponsorships, and staff operations.

Traditional orphanages in Pakistan rely on paper records and manual processes. KafalatCare changes this by combining a well-structured database with practical, impactful AI — giving children better care, sponsors deeper connection, and donors greater transparency.

كفالة + Care

Kafalat (كفالة) — Arabic/Urdu for sponsorship & guardianship of an orphan. In Islamic tradition, caring for an orphan holds immense spiritual significance. KafalatCare honours that tradition while bringing it into the digital age.

What KafalatCare Offers

9 Integrated Modules,
One Unified Platform

From daily meal records to AI-powered risk detection — every aspect of orphanage management in one place.

01

Child Profile Management

Full profile: name, age, medical history, education, legal documents, photos & daily notes — all in one digital record.

02

Health & Medical Records

Track vaccinations, doctor visits, medications, and health alerts. AI automatically flags concerning patterns.

03

Education Tracking

School enrolment, grades, attendance, tutoring sessions, and academic progress — tracked over time with personalised insights.

04

Donation Management

Accept one-time or recurring general donations, issue receipts, allocate funds, and show transparent impact to every donor.

05

Staff & HR Management

Staff profiles, attendance, shift scheduling, salary records, and performance tracking in one place.

08

AI Assistant Module

Staff chatbot, predictive child risk scoring, smart document search & auto-generated monthly sponsorship reports — powered by Machine Learning.

05

Child Sponsorship System

AI-matched monthly child sponsorship. Sponsors track food, health, education & activities daily with full fund transparency.

07

Admin Dashboard & Reports

Real-time statistics, charts, exportable reports, and AI-generated insights supporting data-driven management decisions.

09

Audit & Security Module

Full audit log of every action, role-based access control, encrypted records, and breach detection — keeping children's data safe.
